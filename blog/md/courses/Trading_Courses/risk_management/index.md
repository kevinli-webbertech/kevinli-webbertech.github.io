# Risk Management Deep Dive

A curated index of risk-management resources for day trading — position sizing, trade-level risk/reward,
discipline rules, and the tools used to check them. Start here, then drill into a topic below.

This is the companion deep-dive series to [Class 7 - Risk Management](../class7_risk_management.html).

> **Disclaimer:** Nothing here is personalized financial advice. Figures, percentages, and tool outputs
> are general guidelines/examples — confirm against your own account size, broker rules, and risk
> tolerance before trading.

## Position & Account Risk

* [Position Sizing for Day Trading](position_sizing.html) — core
  risk-per-trade rules and the position-size formula for stocks, futures, and options.
* [Python Risk Calculator](python_risk_calculator.html) — CLI tool implementing
  those formulas (stock, ATR, futures, options, plus batch CSV processing and a daily risk-cap check).
* [Risk-Adjusted Return Ratios](risk_adjusted_return_ratios.html) — Sharpe,
  Sortino, Omega, Calmar, and Martin ratios explained.
