# Class 3 - Trend Analysis

## Goal

Identify the direction of the market using support, resistance, and trendlines.

## Topics

- Uptrends, downtrends, and sideways (range-bound) markets
- Support and resistance levels
- Drawing trendlines correctly
- Trend channels
- Breakouts and false breakouts

## Key Takeaways

- "The trend is your friend" - trading with the trend improves win rate.
- Support and resistance are zones, not exact lines.
- A breakout needs volume confirmation to be reliable.

## Practice

On a daily chart, draw two trendlines connecting recent swing highs and swing lows. Mark the nearest support and resistance zones.
