# Class 3 - Trend Analysis

## Goal

Identify the direction of the market using support, resistance, and trendlines.

## Topics

- Uptrends, downtrends, and sideways (range-bound) markets
- Support and resistance levels
- Drawing trendlines correctly
- Trend channels
- Breakouts and false breakouts

## Definitions

- **Uptrend**: A price pattern of higher highs and higher lows, indicating sustained buying pressure.
- **Downtrend**: A price pattern of lower highs and lower lows, indicating sustained selling pressure.
- **Sideways (range-bound) market**: A market moving between a fairly stable support and resistance level with no clear up or down trend.
- **Support**: A price level or zone where buying pressure has historically been strong enough to stop or reverse a decline.
- **Resistance**: A price level or zone where selling pressure has historically been strong enough to stop or reverse an advance.
- **Trendline**: A straight line drawn connecting a series of swing highs or swing lows to visualize the direction and slope of a trend.
- **Trend channel**: Two parallel trendlines (one along the highs, one along the lows) that contain price action within a trend.
- **Breakout**: A move where price closes beyond a support, resistance, or trendline level, often signaling the start of a new trend.
- **False breakout**: A breakout that fails to continue in the breakout direction and quickly reverses back inside the prior range.

## Key Takeaways

- "The trend is your friend" - trading with the trend improves win rate.
- Support and resistance are zones, not exact lines.
- A breakout needs volume confirmation to be reliable.
