# Class 8 - Trading Psychology

## Goal

Understand the mental discipline required to follow a trading plan consistently.

## Topics

- Fear and greed cycles
- Revenge trading and overtrading
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Building and following a trading plan
- Keeping a trading journal

## Key Takeaways

- Most trading losses come from emotional decisions, not lack of strategy.
- A written trading plan removes in-the-moment decision-making.
- A trading journal helps identify and correct repeated mistakes.

## Practice

Write a one-page trading plan including entry rules, exit rules, and maximum risk per trade.
