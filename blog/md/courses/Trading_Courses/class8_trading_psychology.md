# Class 8 - Trading Psychology

## Goal

Understand the mental discipline required to follow a trading plan consistently.

## Topics

- Fear and greed cycles
- Revenge trading and overtrading
- FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Building and following a trading plan
- Keeping a trading journal

## Definitions

- **Fear and greed cycle**: The recurring emotional pattern where greed drives traders to chase gains near market tops and fear drives them to sell near market bottoms.
- **Revenge trading**: Entering impulsive, often oversized trades to try to immediately recover a recent loss, usually leading to further losses.
- **Overtrading**: Placing too many trades, often from boredom or emotion rather than a valid setup, which increases costs and risk.
- **FOMO (fear of missing out)**: The anxiety-driven urge to enter a trade late because a price move is already underway, often resulting in poor entries.
- **Trading plan**: A written set of rules covering entries, exits, position sizing, and risk management that a trader commits to following.
- **Trading journal**: A log of past trades, including rationale, outcome, and lessons learned, used to identify patterns and improve discipline.

## Key Takeaways

- Most trading losses come from emotional decisions, not lack of strategy.
- A written trading plan removes in-the-moment decision-making.
- A trading journal helps identify and correct repeated mistakes.

## Practice

Write a one-page trading plan including entry rules, exit rules, and maximum risk per trade.
