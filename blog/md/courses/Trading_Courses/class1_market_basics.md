# Class 1 - Market Basics

## Goal

Understand how financial markets work before touching a chart or placing a trade.

## Topics

- What is a stock, an exchange, and a ticker symbol
- Market participants: retail traders, institutions, market makers
- Order types: market order, limit order, stop order
- Bid/ask spread and how trades get filled
- Market hours, pre-market, and after-hours trading
- Brokerage accounts: cash vs. margin accounts

## Key Takeaways

- Every trade has a buyer and a seller matched through the exchange's order book.
- Limit orders give you price control; market orders give you speed.
- Understanding order types is the foundation for every strategy covered later in this course.

## Practice

Open a demo/paper trading account and place a simulated market order and a limit order on the same stock. Compare the fill price of each.
